What I do, one planet at a time.

z

Planet Claude

01

Claude's done? I jump.

“You went for tea. Claude finished 10 minutes ago. Not on my watch.”

Jumps when Claude Code finishes or needs your answer. Click Go to jump to its window. No Claude Code? I still work, just less jumpy.

Planet Localhost

02

Your server died. I noticed.

“localhost:3000 stopped breathing. Was it Ctrl+C? I'm not judging. Okay, a little.”

Checks your dev servers every 5 seconds and your staging or live sites every minute. Tells you when they go down, and when they're back.

Clipboard Moon

03

I remember what you copied.

“That link you copied an hour ago and then overwrote? I kept it.”

Click any old copy to use it again. Passwords from password managers? I look away.

Planet Hydrate

04

Drink water. I'm serious.

“20 minutes of staring at code. Look at something far away. No, not the other monitor.”

Gentle nudges for water, eyes, posture and a short walk. They pause while you're away.

Planet Toasty

05

Your laptop is sweating. So am I.

“CPU at 98% for 3 minutes. Something is stuck, and it's getting toasty in here.”

Warns once when your disk is almost full, the battery is low or the CPU is maxed out, then goes quiet once it's fixed.

Planet Feelings

06

I have feelings. A few.

“My eyes follow your mouse. I get sleepy at night. Shake me and I get dizzy. Please don't shake me.”

Try it: move your mouse around this page.

In space, no one can read your clipboard.

“No account. No cloud. No tracking. I don't even know your name. Hi, stranger.”

  • Runs entirely on your computer
  • Your clipboard and settings never leave it
  • Only goes online to check the websites you ask me to watch
  • This website has no cookies either. Not even the chocolate kind.

Mac: Open Nattu → click Done → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway

Windows: “Windows protected your PC” → More info → Run anyway
